# h1

## Summary
The alternative hypothesis of a test

## Description
Gives the alternative hypothesis a test's result printed, as the sentence of its `H1:` line,
for a report's own text; under `alternative: less` or `greater` it is the directional sentence
the test chose. A result that prints no hypotheses (`test`, `compare`) gives `NA`.

## Arguments
- t: A test's result.

## Value
A `Str`, or `NA`.

## See also
h0, alternative, summary

## Examples
> let t = dataset(wages).t_test(educ, group: female)
> t.h1()
the population means of educ differ between male and female
> dataset(wages).t_test(educ, group: female, alternative: greater).h1()
the population mean of educ is greater in male than in female
