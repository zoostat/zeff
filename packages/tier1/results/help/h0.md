# h0

## Summary
The null hypothesis of a test

## Description
Gives the null hypothesis a test's result printed, as the sentence of its `H0:` line, for a
report's own text. A result that prints no hypotheses (`test`, `compare`) gives `NA`.

## Arguments
- t: A test's result.

## Value
A `Str`, or `NA`.

## See also
h1, alternative, summary

## Examples
> let t = dataset(wages).t_test(educ, group: female)
> t.h0()
the population means of educ are equal in male and female
