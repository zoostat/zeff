# Zeff 0.6.0 — Panels

Released 6 November 2026. The sixth release of the Zeff engine is the one for panel research:
frames are joined, reshaped, aggregated and saved; a frame declares its panel and takes lags,
leads and differences within each unit; `reg` absorbs fixed effects with `fe:` and reads an
event study from `event()` in its formula; dates and times are values a script computes with.
Under them the engine gains grouped computation, shared columns with selection vectors, column
expressions compiled into one pass, and a checker that knows every frame's columns before a
script runs. 37 functions, each with its complete help page: the `time` package (14), `format`
as the first function of `text`, twenty frame and column functions in `data`, `event` in
`regress` and `table` in `core`. What the release unlocks: panel research. The sample project
`samples/panel/` is the release's analysis: the wages merged with their firms, a wide sales
file reshaped, aggregated and saved, a declared panel with lags and differences, three
regressions with firm and year effects in one `report`, an event study on a staggered policy
and a two-period difference-in-differences.

## What shipped

**Dates and times.** `Date` and `DateTime` are computable types: the six comparisons, `Date −
Date` as whole days, `case` over a date column, `to` from and to text with or without a format
and between dates and times, and `min`, `max`, `sorted`, `unique`, `is_in`, `equal` and
`coalesce` on dates. Every other arithmetic on a date is refused with the function to use
(Z0221 naming `add_days`, `add_months` or `days_between`), and a date never compares with text
(Z0210). The `time` package: `date` and `datetime` from text; the parts `year`, `month`, `day`,
`weekday` (1 Monday to 7 Sunday), `quarter` and `week` (the ISO week); `add_days` and
`add_months` (31 January plus one month is the last day of February); `days_between` and
`months_between`; `today()` and `now()`, which read the machine's clock and say so in a note
with the value at each use (W0703). `format(x, digits:, big_mark:, pattern:)` turns a number or
a date into text.

**Joins, reshaping, aggregation and saving.** `merge(frame, on:, join:, relation:, suffixes:)`
with a note of the matched and unmatched rows on each side, the stated relation checked before
joining (Z0603) and shared columns that are not keys refused without `suffixes:` (Z0604);
`append` by column name, a column absent on one side filled with `NA` and named in a note;
`to_long` and `to_wide`, which round-trip (`sales_2010` from `sales` and `2010`); `aggregate(name
= expr, …, by:)`, one row per group in key order with `NA` last; `distinct`, `duplicates` (a
table of the repeated keys with their counts) and `sample` (its seed recorded in a note, the
kept rows in their original order); `save` to CSV and TSV under the writing rules of section
3.8, the written bytes identical on every target. `table(title:, columns:)` builds a printed
table from lists or columns.

**Panels.** `panel(unit:, time:)` declares a frame's panel, refuses a repeated time within a
unit and a missing unit or time (Z0601), builds its index once and prints `panel worker × year`
in the header of `describe`. The declaration survives the verbs that keep both columns and is
dropped with a note by those that do not. On a panel, `lag`, `lead` and `diff` look periods
back, not rows, so a gap gives `NA` as Stata's `L.` does (Z0602 on a frame without a
declaration); `fill_forward` fills within units. Beside them `running_sum`, `running_product`,
`rank` with four tie rules, `bin` (an ordered category labelled as R's `cut` labels it, a value
outside every interval `NA` with W0210), `quantile_group` and `winsorize`.

**Fixed effects in `reg`.** `fe: firm + year` absorbs any number of fixed effects and their
interactions (`a:b`). Rows with a missing effect are dropped and counted, then singletons,
repeatedly until none remains; the outcome and the regressors are projected off the effects by
alternating projections with acceleration to a tolerance of 1e-8, in the order written; a
regressor the effects absorb is named in a note and dropped, and one left with under one per
cent of its variation is warned about (W1004). Degrees of freedom count the absorbed levels,
effects nested in the clusters left out, which reproduces the defaults of fixest and reghdfe.
The printed fit carries the effects with their numbers of levels, the singletons, and R² within
and overall with their adjusted forms; `report` gains its `Fixed effects` row and
`r_squared_within`; `fitted` and `predict` include the effects' part; group indexes and
projected columns are reused within a run, so a robustness table that shares `fe:` and rows
projects once. `event(time, treat, ref:)` is the event-study term of a formula: one indicator
per period relative to treatment, the reference period left out, never-treated units as the
controls, the terms named `event=-3`, `event=0`, `event=2`.

**The checker's schema pass.** `zeff check`, and `zeff run` before it runs anything, now know
the columns and types of every frame: the header and first 1,000 rows of an imported file
(cached in `.zeff/schemas/` and refreshed when the file's size or time changes), a shipped
dataset, a `frame` literal, and the columns after every verb. A misnamed variable in any `Var`,
`Vars`, `Terms` or `Formula` argument, in a definition or a condition, or handed to a project
function is reported with "did you mean" before the first statement runs. What cannot be known
before running (`to_wide`, an import of a file not yet written) is marked "checked at run time",
and after a run the columns it produced serve the next check as warnings marked "from last
run". A file under a data root is hashed as it is read, and a change since the last run warns
(W0701).

**Memory and speed, with no result changed.** `where`, `sort` and `sample` record the kept rows
and share the parent's columns, gathering a column the first time it is read; `zeff run` frees
a binding after its last use; `zeff run --verbose` prints the memory record. An expression over
columns inside a frame method is compiled into one pass over the rows in blocks of 4,096 with no
temporary columns; the earlier evaluation stays in the engine as the oracle, and a property test
holds the two to the same bits and the same messages. Every digest of the benchmark suite and
every earlier line of the conformance report is unchanged by either.

**Datasets.** The shipped `wages` gains its eleventh variable, `firm`, without a change to any
value of the ten it had, and `firms` ships beside it: one row per firm with its industry, size
and founding date, three of them employing no worker.

**Diagnostics.** 127 codes, 13 more than 0.5.0: Z0210 (a date compared with text), Z0507
(`event`'s reference period absent from the data), Z0601 and Z0602 (panels), Z0603 and Z0604
(`merge`), Z0609 (`append`), Z0610 and Z0611 (reshaping), W0210 (`bin`), W0701 (a changed data
file), W0703 (the clock) and W1004 (little variation left after the fixed effects), each with a
snapshot of its wording and an explained page behind `zeff help <code>`. The JSON output carries
the schema pass's two marks for the language server of 0.7.0.

**Testing.** Thirteen new R reference scripts: eleven in base R, exact, for the dates, `format`,
the panel and running functions, the verbs and the saved files, and two on fixest for `fe:` and
`event`, within 1e-7. A sixth sample project; script tests of the statements of sections 6, 6.1
and 7.1 of the Language Reference; a storage round trip of every new frame with its panel
declaration; the fuzz targets extended to dates, formats, the new verbs' arguments and `fe:`
terms; the weekly mutation job extended to the projection kernel and the fixed-effects code,
with a fixed seed for its property tests; two new benchmark cases (two-way fixed effects and a
chain of column expressions); 46 new conformance cases, 194 in all. 1,160 tests run on every
push.

## Breaking changes

- **`table` became a function of `core`**, which a script cannot rebind (Z0105). A 0.5.0 script
  with `let table = …` is refused; the rewrite is to rename the binding.
- **`wages` has eleven variables.** `n_cols`, `variables` and `describe` on it show `firm` as
  the last column, and the import note reads "11 variables". No value of the ten earlier columns
  changed, so every statistic and every fit on them is what it was. A script that counted the
  columns, or selected "to the last column", names the columns it wants instead.
- **`ci()` on a fit made at another level gives intervals at the fit's level** (open decision
  #67), so that it agrees with the printed summary; at 0.5.0 it gave 95 per cent whatever the
  fit's level. A fit made at the default level is unaffected. The rewrite, to keep the old
  intervals: `m.ci(level: 0.95)`.
- **A misnamed column stops a script before it starts.** `zeff run` now reports the error
  before the first statement runs; 0.5.0 ran the statements before the faulty one and then
  stopped. A script that ran to its end is unaffected.
- **The new names of `time`, `text`, `data` and `regress`** (`year`, `month`, `day`, `date`,
  `week`, `rank`, `sample`, `lag`, `format`, `event` and the rest) may still be bound with
  `let`, with the warning W0101 that the binding hides a function; a script run under
  `--deny-warnings` renames the binding.
- **The project's pin.** A project names the exact engine it runs on, so a 0.5.0 project sets
  `zeff = "0.6.0"` in its `zeff.toml` (Z0710 says so otherwise), as at every release until the
  launcher of 0.9.0.

No other printed result of a 0.5.0 script changes: the 148 conformance cases of 0.5.0 kept
their digests through the release, except three: the printed pages of the two sample projects
that describe `wages` or read it from its file, where the eleventh variable shows.

## Known issues

See `docs/known_issues.md`. New at this release: `save` writes CSV and TSV only; a fit under
`fe:` has no leverage, so `se: hc2`, `se: hc3` and `influence` are refused there until 0.8.0;
the fixed effects are projected out by an iteration with a tolerance of 1e-8 and a cap of
10,000 passes; a fixed-effects fit runs on one thread, about twice fixest's time; `event` needs
units treated at different times, or never; a panel's time is an Int or a `Date`, and a `Date`
counts days; dates and times carry no zone and no duration; `today()` and `now()` are recorded
only by their note; the checker does not know a frame's columns after `to_wide` or after an
import of a file not yet written; `merge` refuses a many-to-many relation, and `append` and
`aggregate` have their rules; `winsorize` warns twice and names `value`; three more functions
planned in Zeff are written in Rust (`diff`, `quantile_group`, `duplicates`); eight conditions
carry codes numbered at this release. The mutation-testing and browser-bundle entries carry
this release's figures, and the kernel's mean of values that cancel was reviewed and stands as
a documented property. Resolved: a `Var` parameter of a project function is checked at `zeff
check`.
