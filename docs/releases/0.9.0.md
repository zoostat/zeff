# Release 0.9.0 — Binary outcomes

The row of section 14 of the Engine and Project Specification: 6 functions, `logistic`, `probit`, `margins`, `roc`, `confusion` and `timer`, which open decision #112 (the owner's, 11 November 2026, option A) turns into 5 before anything is built: `glm(y ~ x, family: binomial, link: logit | probit = logit)` with `family:` required in the place of `logistic` and `probit`, and `fit.curve(newdata:, type: roc)` in the place of `fit.roc()`; `margins`, `confusion` and `timer` are unchanged (catalogue rows 260, 262, 263, 515, 516 and 64). Two hard engine pieces. The first is the likelihood trait and the shared optimiser, with IRLS and Newton fitting, separation checks, robust and clustered errors from the variance layer, and marginal effects with delta-method standard errors for `reg` and `glm`. The second is the split of the `zeff` command into a small launcher and versioned engines, with `zeff engine`, `zeff doctor`, signed engine downloads, the known-issues list as data and `zeff audit`, which patch releases need from their first day (Engine Specification, section 2.1; Language Reference, section 11.1). What it unlocks: binary-outcome studies, and patch releases (0.9.1 and after). After it 266 of the 279 functions of 1.0 are implemented, if the two new functions of #165 and #167 are taken (277 in 1.0 after the merge of #112, 279 with them).

## Rulings taken from the specification (engineering; each recorded in `docs/decisions.md` in the step that needs it)

- **Every address the launcher trusts is under `zeff-lang.dev`.** Recorded at this planning (`docs/decisions.md`, 20 November 2026, "Start release 0.9.0"). The launcher compiles in one host, `https://zeff-lang.dev/`, and two paths under it: the signed key list and the signed index of engines. The address of every archive is read from the index, never built from a path of a GitHub organisation; `ZEFF_MIRROR` (or `[network] mirror`) replaces the host and nothing else; the built-in public key, not the host, decides what is trusted. A test of the launcher's crate fails if any `github.com` address, or the name of any organisation, stands in its sources or its binary. The repository stays under `zoostat`; a move to another organisation at 0.11.0 changes the index alone and breaks no installed launcher. This holds under either answer to question 4, so #31 does not return to the owner before the launcher's step.
- **The launcher sends nothing but its request.** No telemetry (Zemp Specification, sections 9 and 13); the anonymous count of engine fetches of #116 is the server's, without the machine's address kept.
- `glm` is a new tier-2 package, `packages/tier2/glm`, beside `regress`, depending on `results` and the kernels only (section 8.1, rule 5: no model package depends on another). The likelihood trait lives in `results` beside the estimator trait; the optimiser's numerics (IRLS by weighted QR, Newton with a step-halving line search, the convergence rule) in `zeff-kernels`, pure Rust, in the fixed reduction shape, proved identical on the four targets. A binary fit starts from the linear-probability fit (section 8.3's table of warm starts). Convergence is on the relative change of the deviance and of the largest coefficient, to a tolerance tighter than R's default, so that R's figures are matched within the iterative class; the iterations are part of the result and of `--verbose`.
- **Standard errors.** Classical errors from the expected information (the inverse of X′WX at the solution, as R's `glm` gives); `se: robust`, `hc2`, `hc3` and `cluster:` from the variance layer with the scores and bread of the likelihood, as `sandwich` on a `glm` gives them; `weights:` as frequency weights; `fe:` in `glm` refused with a message naming release 1.7 (`glm` with fixed effects and PPML), unless the owner's answer to #163 brings it earlier.
- **Separation.** Checked before the fit: complete and quasi-complete separation found by a linear-programming check on the design (Konis's method, as R's `detectseparation` uses), reported as Z0803 in the wording of section 11 of the Language Reference ("`female` perfectly predicts `union` (separation)"); fitted probabilities numerically 0 or 1 after convergence a W1 warning with its row, page and snapshot. Self-checks after every fit (section 11.1): the log-likelihood never fell, the score is zero at the solution, a symmetric positive semi-definite variance matrix.
- **`margins`** is a method of the estimator interface in `results` (section 8.1, rule 5), implemented by `reg` and `glm`: the model matrix records, for each column, which variables it is a function of and how, so that the derivative is taken through `exper^2`, `log(x)` and an interaction exactly, by the chain rule on the formula's terms (#195); the delta method's Jacobian is analytic where the term is and a central difference otherwise, the choice recorded per term. `ivreg` is not given `margins` at 1.0 (the catalogue names `reg`, `logistic` and `probit`).
- **`curve` and `confusion`** live in `glm` as result methods of a binary fit. The area under the ROC curve is the Mann–Whitney statistic of the predicted probabilities, computed exactly; the curve's points are every distinct threshold; ties are counted half. `confusion` at `cutoff: 0.5` by default (the catalogue's usage).
- **`timer(expr)`** in `core`, scripts only (Z0110), runs its expression and prints the elapsed time as a note; the time is read from the clock the run is given through the file-system interface, fixed in tests and in the conformance cases, as `today()` and `now()` are.
- **The launcher and the engines.** A new crate `crates/zeff-launcher`, a small binary with few dependencies, and the engine's `zeff` binary unchanged in behaviour when it is run directly. The store and its layout are section 2.1's; an install unpacks into a temporary folder of the store and renames it into place, so an interrupted install leaves nothing half-made. Archives are `tar.zst` (or `.zip` on Windows) with `SHA256SUMS` and a minisign (ed25519) signature, verified before unpacking (Z0711). Each dependency (the archive, compression, signature and HTTPS crates) is chosen at its step for a permissive licence, pinned and recorded in `docs/decisions.md`; the WebAssembly build links none of them.
- **The known-issues list as data.** Each engine ships its known wrong-result issues as a versioned data file (the engine versions, functions and options each affects, and the version that fixes it), built from `docs/known_issues.md`'s entries that are wrong results; `zeff audit` reads the project's run records against the list of the newest engine installed, and says plainly when a pinned version is affected (Language Reference, section 11.1).
- **The plots handed over (#197, if taken).** `zeff-web` keeps the plots of a run as it keeps frames; a call lays one out at a size in points and a theme and returns the SVG, without a run; the scene is handed over as data. The bundle and its cold start are measured against R4 at the step.
- **Codes.** Codes the text gives are used as given (Z0803, Z0709 to Z0711, Z0110); conditions it describes without a number receive provisional codes with their rows in section 11's table, logged, as before. The removed names are refused (`d.logistic(...)`, `d.logit(...)`, `d.probit(...)` and `fit.roc()`) by the checker with the rewrite to `glm` or `curve`, under the code of the merges of 0.8.0.
- **Catalogue and counts.** Rows 262 and 263 become one row `glm`, row 515 becomes `curve` (#112); new rows for `n_chars` and `row_number` if taken; each row marked implemented in the step that ships it; the counts of sections 8.2, 9.2 and 14 follow at each step and are logged.
- **Help pages.** Every page of this release is written to the help contract of 0.8.0; the 84 pages of `data`, `datasets`, `dist` and `stats` are revised as the 94 of 0.8.0 were, by the method recorded at step 22a, nothing copied from another program's documentation.
- **Not pulled forward.** The installer script, Homebrew, winget, Scoop, the container image, the GitHub Action, `zeff self update` and reproducible official builds (1.0.0); `zeff export replication` and `zeff verify` (0.11.0); `zeff app` (0.11.0); `poly` (1.2); further families and links of `glm` (1.7); odds ratios and the other options of question 3 of #99 (after 1.0); C23 to C27 of #175 (0.11.0 if question 5 is answered as recommended); the website (1.0.0).
