# Zeff 0.7.0 — Tests, plots and the language server

Released 15 November 2026. The seventh release of the Zeff engine is the one for exploratory
analysis: a first look at data can be tabulated, tested and drawn. Nine tests with `anova` in
its one-way and factorial forms, `posthoc` and `p_adjust`; a layered grammar of plots, with its
shorthands, written as SVG, PDF and PNG by the engine's own renderer, the same bytes on every
machine; twelve text functions; functions as values over lists. Beside them the tools an
editor and an interface need: a language server (diagnostics as one types, completion,
signature help, hover, formatting), a tree-sitter grammar, `zeff fmt`, `zeff test`,
`zeff completions`, and the engine as a worker that reports its progress, can be cancelled
and keeps a memory budget, natively and in a browser. 72 functions, each with its complete
help page: `plot` (35), `text` (12), `stats` (11), six methods of a test's result and `design`
on a fit, `tabulate`, `dist.tukey`, and `note`, `name_of`, `map`, `filter` and `reduce` in
`core`. What the release unlocks: exploratory analysis, and the start of Zemp's interface.
Two sample projects are the release's analyses: `samples/tests/`, nine questions asked of the
shipped wages and each answered by the test that fits it, and `samples/plots/`, fifteen
figures of the same data.

## What shipped

**Tests.** `t_test` (one mean, two groups by Welch's test or with equal variances, paired),
`wilcoxon_test` (signed-rank and rank-sum, exact below 50 a group with or without ties, as R
4.6.0), `proportion_test` (Wilson's, Wald's and the exact interval; Newcombe's for a
difference), `chi2_test` (independence and goodness of fit), `fisher_test` (the conditional
odds ratio with its interval on 2 × 2, the p-value on larger tables), `kruskal_test`,
`shapiro_test` and `ks_test` (exact, asymptotic, and Lilliefors' form with the parameters
estimated). Each prints its hypotheses in words and in symbols, the estimate with its
interval, the statistic, the p-value, an effect size where one is usual, and a decision line
at α = 1 − `level:`; `alternative:` gives the one-sided forms. A test's result is read as
values: `statistic()`, `df()`, `p_values()`, `effect_size()`, `h0()`, `h1()`, `alternative()`,
beside `estimates()`, `ci()` and `n_obs()`.

**Analysis of variance.** `anova(y, group: g)` is Welch's F unless `equal_var: true`;
`anova(y ~ a * b + x)` is the factorial table, type 2 by default as `car::Anova`, type 3 with
sum contrasts, type 1 as `anova(lm())`, with a covariate allowed among the terms and frequency
or analytic weights. `effect:` chooses eta, omega or epsilon squared, partial in the factorial
form, and `omega_f` the partial omega squared computed from F as Stata documents it. One row
of the table is read by its name: `p_values("dose")`, `statistic("dose:diet")`.

**Comparisons after it.** `posthoc` compares the group means of a one-way `anova`, or the
estimated marginal means of a term after a formula, by Tukey's HSD, Scheffé's method, or the
pairwise t-tests adjusted by Bonferroni, Šidák, Holm, Hochberg, Hommel, Benjamini and
Hochberg or Benjamini and Yekutieli; the single-step methods give intervals at `level:`.
`p_adjust` adjusts a list of p-values, or a column inside `add` with families by `by:`.
`dist.tukey` is the studentized range's distribution, computed to the accuracy class of the
other distributions, which R's `ptukey` does not reach.

**Tables.** `tabulate` gives frequencies and percentages of one variable or two, with row,
column or total percentages.

**Plots.** `data.plot(x:, y:, color:, ...)` starts a layered plot and the marks are added to
it: `points`, `lines`, `step`, `bars`, `area`, `ribbon`, `text`, `tile`, `rug`, `hline`,
`vline`; the statistical layers `histogram`, `density`, `boxplot` and `errorbars`; then
`scale_x`, `scale_y` (linear, log, square root, dates) and `scale` (colours, fills, sizes,
shapes; the Okabe–Ito palette for categories and viridis for numbers), `coord` (flipped,
polar, fixed), `facet` (wrapped or in a grid, with free scales), `labels`, `legend`, `theme`
(journal, minimal, slide) and `annotate`. An expression may stand on an axis: `y: log(wage)`.
A plot prints as a table of its axes, scales and layers, and `export` writes it as SVG, PDF
or PNG at the size of one journal column, two, a slide, or a size given. Eight shorthands draw
the common figures in one call: `histogram`, `scatter` (with `fit: linear`), `line`, `bar`,
`boxplot`, `density`, `qq_plot`, `heatmap`. A fit draws itself: `coef_plot()`, and `plot()`
of an event study. The renderer is the engine's own: text in IBM Plex, set by the engine's
own measures, and every byte of every file the same on Linux, macOS and in a browser.

**Text.** `upper`, `lower`, `trim`, `contains`, `starts_with`, `ends_with`, `replace`,
`extract`, `split`, `join`, `slice` and `pad`, over one text or a column, with patterns as
regular expressions where asked.

**The language.** A function is a value where one is expected: `map(xs, fn(x) x * 2)`,
`filter` and `reduce`, with a named function or one written on the spot. `note(message)`
beside `warn`, both with `code:`; `name_of(v)`. A warning is allowed on one statement by a
pragma, `// zeff: allow(W0201) reason`, and `--deny-warnings` refuses the rest. Inside the
body of a function the function's own names come first, whatever the variables of the frame
it is given are called. A function of a script calls the functions its `use` lines brought in
by their short names. A loop over a range takes one number at a pass and lists nothing.
`design()` gives the model matrix of a fit as a frame.

**Tools.** `zeff fmt` writes every script of a project in the one style of the language, and
`--check` says whether any would change. `zeff test` runs every `assert` of a project and
every example of its functions' pages. `zeff lsp` is the language server: the checker's
messages as one types, completion that knows a frame's variables and an option's words,
signature help, hover with a function's help page or a variable's type, label and missing
count, and formatting. `editors/tree-sitter-zeff` is the grammar for editors that colour by
it. `zeff completions bash | zsh | fish | powershell` writes the completion file of a shell.
An error raised inside a function says which calls it came through.

**The engine as a worker.** A run tells each statement and each pass of a loop as it goes;
`zeff run` prints as each statement ends and stops on Ctrl-C between two steps (Z0705). A
memory budget, `memory` in `zeff.toml` or `--memory`, refuses a statement whose estimate
passes it before it starts (Z0704). In a browser the same engine runs in a Web Worker, built
for WebAssembly: a run with its progress, cancelled through shared memory, a frame passed to
the page as an Arrow stream without a copy.

**Corrected.** `mad`, `skewness` and `kurtosis` gave a wrong number in 0.5.0 and 0.6.0 on a
frame that had a variable called `centre` (see Breaking changes). The seed the engine notes
for an unseeded run now repeats that run when written as `seed(n)` at the top. Six values of
the beta quantile at extreme parameters were wrong and are right.

## Breaking changes

- **A script that begins `seed(n)` and then draws gives other numbers than under 0.3.0 to
  0.6.0.** Draws are now keyed by the place of their statement among the statements that
  draw, so that the seed the engine notes for an unseeded run repeats that run when written
  at the top. A draw with a `seed:` of its own is unchanged. There is no rewrite that restores
  the old numbers; a stored output that depended on them is renewed once.
- **Wrong results of `mad`, `skewness` and `kurtosis` are corrected** (releases 0.5.0 and
  0.6.0). They were wrong only where the frame had a variable called `centre`, or where the
  variable given to one of the two moments was called `dev2`; every affected run printed the
  warning W0102 or a note that a variable had been replaced. `mad` of 1, 2, 3, 9 in such a
  frame printed 5.1891 and prints 1.4826. Elsewhere the three give the same bits as before.
- **Values too large to square are refused** by `t_test`, the one-way `anova` and Pearson's
  `correlation` (Z0803, the code `reg` already used): a variable with a value above 1e150 in
  size. `correlation` printed 0 for such a variable from 0.3.0 to 0.6.0, which was wrong. The
  rewrite is the help line's: divide the variable by a power of ten first.
- **The new names.** A script's function named as one of the release's functions (`step`,
  `plot`, `points`, `lines`, `text`, `density`, `scatter`, `filter`, `map`, `reduce`, `note`,
  `split`, `join`, `slice`, `pad` and the rest of the list above) is refused with Z0109; the
  rewrite is a new name for the script's function. `map`, `filter`, `reduce`, `note` and
  `name_of` are names of `core`, which a `let` cannot rebind (Z0105); a `let` of another
  package's new name is allowed with the warning W0101. An argument called `step:` or
  `filter:` is untouched.
- **Every warning and note that has a code prints it**, as `warning[W0204]: log of zero gave
  -Inf (line 4)`. No result changes; a script whose standard error is compared with a stored
  copy renews the copy. The too-few-values warnings of `skewness` and `kurtosis` carry W0205
  and new wording.
- **`estimates()` has two more columns**, `df` and `df2`, after `statistic`. A script that
  read the columns of that frame by position names them instead.
- **A project that sets `memory` in its `zeff.toml` is now held to it** (the key was read
  since 0.1.0 and never applied): a statement estimated to need more is refused with Z0704
  before it starts. The rewrite is a larger figure, or the key removed.
- **A comment that begins `// zeff:` must be a valid pragma** (Z0317 otherwise); it was an
  ordinary comment.
- **An expression where a variable is named** (`w.mean(wage * 2)`, `by: a + b`) is answered
  with Z0401 and the advice to compute it with `add` first, where 0.6.0 gave Z0101 or a
  message about types. It was refused before and is refused now; only the code differs.
- **`quantile_group`'s argument errors print as Z0001**, where 0.6.0 gave Z0406 and Z0401
  (the function is now written in Zeff; open decision #89 restores the codes at 0.8.0).
- **`zeff run` prints as it goes and can be interrupted.** A statement's output appears when
  the statement ends; Ctrl-C stops the run with Z0705 and exit code 1. An error raised inside
  a function also prints the calls that led to it, as `= in:` lines.
- **Twelve of the shipped scripts were reformatted by `zeff fmt`**, so the line numbers in
  the standard error of three sample projects moved.
- **The project's pin.** A 0.6.0 project sets `zeff = "0.7.0"` in its `zeff.toml` (Z0710 says
  so otherwise), as at every release until the launcher of 0.9.0.

Changes that only let more scripts run: a function's own names come first inside its body, so
a project function no longer meets Z0106 or W0102 because of a frame's variable names (open
decision #140); a script's function may call by its short name a module function brought in
with `use`; `PI` and `E` are the constants inside a frame method; a definition under `by:`
whose result is `NA` in every row gives a Float variable where 0.6.0 refused it; the checker
no longer refuses a later use of the groups of `aggregate(..., by: vars(a, b))`; a loop over a
written range no longer builds its list first, with every result and every draw the same bit
for bit. Six values of the beta quantile at extreme parameters (a = 500,000, b = 0.5) and
one at a = 5,857, b = 12,557 were wrong and are right.

No other printed result of a 0.6.0 script changes: the 194 conformance cases of 0.6.0 kept
their result digests through the release, except the seeded draws named above.

## Known issues

See `docs/known_issues.md`, whose section for this release has 29 entries. The ones a user
meets first: the tests take one variable at a time and no sampling weights; an effect size has
no exact interval yet; `posthoc` has ten methods and no Games-Howell; `shapiro_test` stops at
5,000 values, `fisher_test` at a million tables, the exact `ks_test` at 100 values; only the
two axes of a plot take an expression; a figure is set in IBM Plex, upright only, and its SVG
text is drawn as outlines; the language server has been driven by tests and not yet by an
editor; `zeff fmt` has one style; a run is cancelled between two steps, not inside one; the
memory budget is an estimate that leans high; in a browser the engine needs a page that is
cross-origin isolated; the weekly mutation job's time budget is too small for the four older
packages, whose lists were completed on the development machine at this release; a one-sided
interval of `wilcoxon_test` at exactly 50 per cent is not unique under the normal
approximation, and R's differs; a function of `time` or `text` given a column of a type it does
not take ends in an internal error (open decision #108, 0.8.0); inside a function a parameter
does not stand for a term of a fit (open decision #147, 0.8.0). Resolved at this release: the
examples of a module function's page are run by `zeff test`; a script's function may call a
module's by its short name; a loop over a range no longer builds its list; the wrong results of
`mad`, `skewness` and `kurtosis`; the seed noted for a run repeats it; `memory` in `zeff.toml`
is applied.
