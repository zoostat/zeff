# Zeff 0.5.0 — Language complete

Released 1 November 2026. The fifth release of the Zeff engine completes the language: `if` and
`case`; `for` loops with `break` and `continue` and the `[for ...]` list; `fn` in a script and in
the modules of a project's `functions/` folder, brought in with `use`; any function callable as a
method on its first argument, and functions as values; spreads in calls and records; the `Failed`
type with `attempt`; and `iterate`. With them come the first built-in functions written in Zeff
itself, loaded, checked and documented under the same contract as the Rust ones. 33 functions,
each with its complete help page: fourteen collection functions, `attempt` and `iterate`, and
seventeen planned in Zeff, eleven of them written in it. What the release unlocks: user-written
functions. The sample project `samples/user_functions/` is the release's analysis: two modules of
the project's own functions, a loop over outcomes, subsamples under `attempt` of which one fails,
and Huber's M-estimate of a location by `iterate`.

## What shipped

**Control flow.** `if`, `else if` and `else`, and `case`, as statements and as values, over blocks
whose last expression is their value and whose inner statements print nothing; `case` row by row
inside `add` and `where` once a condition is a column; `for` over lists, ranges and `vars`,
`[for ...]` building a list, `break` and `continue`. The loop pass joins the key of the random
stream, so a draw inside a loop differs by pass and repeats on a re-run, and every draw outside a
loop keeps the published digests of 0.3.0 and 0.4.0.

**Functions.** `fn` in a script with typed parameters, defaults, optional `T?` parameters, word
lists and a checked return type; definitions in any order; a body that sees its parameters and
other functions only, and never itself; a `///` page, a summary line at least in a script and
the complete page in a module. Modules in `functions/*.zeff`, brought in with `use functions::m`
or `use functions::m::name`, private `_names`, and the qualified forms `functions::name` and
`m::name`; a module function runs under its own file, so a failure names `functions/x.zeff:7`,
and `zeff help m::name` prints its page inside the project. Any function may be called as a
method on its first argument (`wages.share_missing(educ)`, `5.abs()`); `Fn(T1, T2) -> R` types
let a function be passed as a value. Spreads: `..opts` in a call and `..r` in a record, an
argument written out overriding the spread's field.

**Failure as a value.** `attempt(expr)` turns a script error into a `Failed` value that carries
its code, message and line. A failed value may stand in a list and reach `is`, `type_of`,
`print`, `report`, `len` and `n_missing`; anywhere else it is refused with the failure it holds
(Z0220). `report` skips failed fits with a warning naming the causes; `if m.is(Failed) { continue }`
is the idiom, and an unguarded method call on a value from `attempt` warns (W0209). Printed:
`failed · Z0801 at line 12: no row remains once rows with NA are dropped`.

**`iterate`.** `iterate(start:, step:, done:, args:, max:, keep:)` calls `step(state, ..args)`
until `done(old, new)` is true or `max:` passes have been made (1,000 by default), and returns
the final state, or every state under `keep: true`, with the number of passes as a note and
W0805 at `max:`; `step` and `done` are checked against `start:` and `args:` at the call. The EM
of section 5.5 of the Language Reference and the M-estimate of the sample are written with it.

**Built-in functions written in Zeff.** A package lists `functions/*.zeff` files; when the engine
starts each is parsed, checked once for every way its scalar parameters may be given as columns,
and lowered; its page is assembled from the `///` lines under the checks a Rust page gets, its
body is held to the tier rules, its "Warnings and notes" are generated from the functions it
calls, and its messages are placed at the call in the script. Eleven functions ship this way:
`clamp`, `logit`, `inv_logit` and `assert` in `core`; `between`, `center`, `standardize`, `iqr`,
`mad`, `skewness` and `kurtosis` in `data`. Seven planned in Zeff are written in Rust for a
piece the language lacks (`fill_missing`, `n_missing`, `row_mean`, `row_sum`, `drop_missing`,
`vif` and `summary`; open decisions #68 to #72), and nothing a script sees differs.

**Collections.** `len`, `first`, `last`, `unique`, `sorted`, `reversed`, `any`, `all`, `append`,
`keys`, `is_in`, `seq`, `vars` and `equal` (open decision #5: structural equality by the identity
relation of section 3.7), on lists that are never changed in place; `any` and `all` three-valued
by the truth table; `seq` as R's `seq.default`, bit for bit; `unique` and `sorted` with `NA` last.

**Diagnostics.** 114 codes, 20 more than 0.4.0 (the function and module codes Z0106 to Z0110,
Z0205, Z0218, Z0225, Z0319, Z0320 and Z0323; the failure codes Z0220 and W0801; the loop and
`case` warnings W0208, W0209 and W0301 to W0303; `seq`'s Z0418; `iterate`'s W0805), each with a
snapshot of its wording and an explained page behind `zeff help <code>`, every one written on the
diagnostics-as-data path, which was the first test of risk R5. Z0304 ("not in this release") now
covers only a `model { }` block, a `dag { }` block, a label written with `as` and a third-party
`use`.

**Testing.** Eleven new R reference files, every one in base R (the collection functions exact
and `seq` bit for bit; `logit` and `inv_logit` within 1e-15; the moments, the scale functions,
the spreads and the row functions within 1e-12; `vif` within 1e-10 against `lm` on the wages and
Wooldridge's wage1); script tests that run the statements of sections 5.1 to 5.5 and the help
document's examples; the checker fuzz target extended to functions, loops, modules and spreads
with a dictionary of the language's words; `zeff-ir` and `zeff-engine` in the weekly coverage
report; the weekly mutation job in 34 shards with a four-minute floor and test builds without
debug information; 41 new conformance cases, 148 in all. 1,019 tests run on every push.

## Breaking changes

Twenty-two names became functions of `core`, which a script cannot rebind (Z0105): `len`,
`first`, `last`, `unique`, `sorted`, `reversed`, `any`, `all`, `append`, `keys`, `is_in`, `seq`,
`vars`, `equal`, `attempt`, `iterate`, `clamp`, `logit`, `inv_logit`, `fill_missing`,
`n_missing` and `assert`. A 0.4.0 script with `let first = …` or `let seq = …` is refused; the
rewrite is to rename the binding. `let center = …`, `let iqr = …` and the other nine new names of
`data` and `regress` still run, with the warning W0101 that they hide a function. A method call
on a value the function does not take now says what it takes instead of "not a method"; no
script that ran changes its meaning. No printed result of a 0.4.0 script changes: the 107
conformance cases of 0.4.0 kept their digests through the release.

## Known issues

See `docs/known_issues.md`. New at this release: a `model { }` block, a `dag { }` block, a label
written with `as` and a third-party `use` are the constructs the grammar has but the release
does not run; a project function is not applied row by row to a column and may not call itself;
a `Var` parameter is checked when the statement runs until the schema pass of 0.6.0, and the
examples of a module function's page are not run until `zeff test` at 0.7.0; `case` over rows
evaluates every arm on the whole frame and refuses a Category, Date or DateTime column as a
value; a failed value reaches six functions only; the pages of built-ins written in Zeff list
more warnings than they can raise, and the "too few values" warning of `skewness` and `kurtosis`
carries no code in `--format json`; seven functions planned in Zeff are written in Rust; the
mean of values that cancel almost exactly is within a rounding of the largest input rather than
of the result; `vif` gives one factor per column of the design; `equal` compares values, not
columns or frames, `sorted` takes lists of scalars, `seq` refuses more than ten million values
and a step of zero or the wrong sign, and `drop_missing` names its variables; `iterate` prints
its number of passes as a note. The mutation-testing and browser-bundle entries carry this
release's figures. Nothing of the earlier lists is resolved at this release.
