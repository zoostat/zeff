# The surviving mutants that have been read and accepted, each with the key of its reason.
#
# The weekly mutation job (.github/workflows/scheduled.yml) compares what each shard leaves with
# this list, through tools/mutants/compare.py, and prints the survivors that are not on it:
# those are the ones to read, each a test to write or a reason to record (testing handbook,
# section 1). The reasons themselves are in docs/decisions.md, under the dates the keys name.
#
# One survivor a line, four fields separated by tabs: the path; the mutation as cargo-mutants
# words it, without the line and column, which move with every edit; how many times it is
# recorded in that routine; and the key of its reason.
