# Z0802 — no residual degrees of freedom

## Meaning
The model has as many regressors as rows, or more, once the collinear columns are dropped, so the fit is exact and nothing is left to estimate the error variance from; or every column of the design is zero and there is no regressor at all. Standard errors, t values and p-values need at least one residual degree of freedom (section 5.6: a model that cannot be estimated).

## Common causes
- A small frame, `head(3)`, with four terms.
- A category whose levels are as many as the rows.

## Example
```
> dataset(wages).head(3).reg(wage ~ educ + exper + female)
error[Z0802]: no residual degrees of freedom: 3 rows and 4 regressors
```

## Fix
Use more rows, or fewer terms.
