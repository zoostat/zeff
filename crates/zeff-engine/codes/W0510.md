# W0510 — a regressor takes away part of the effect

## Meaning
`check` held a fitted model against a causal graph and found a regressor that lies on a causal path from the exposure to the outcome, or below a variable that does. Part of the effect of the exposure reaches the outcome through that variable; with it held fixed, the coefficient of the exposure is no longer the total effect but what is left of it. A regressor below the outcome itself is warned of under the same code: holding a consequence of the outcome fixed distorts the estimate. The fit is not changed and not refused: the warning names the regressor and the path.

## Common causes
- A mediator among the controls: a variable the exposure acts through, added because it "explains" the outcome.
- A variable measured after the exposure and shaped by it, or by a mediator.
- A graph whose arrow points the wrong way: the regressor is in truth a cause of the exposure.

## Example
```
> let g = dag {
>     training -> productivity
>     training -> tenure -> productivity
> }
> let m = dataset(staff).reg(productivity ~ training + tenure)
> let c = m.check(g, exposure: training)
warning[W0510]: tenure lies on the path training → tenure → productivity
  = help: adjusting for a mediator removes part of the effect of training; drop tenure to estimate the total effect
```

## Fix
For the total effect, fit the model without the regressor. If the effect that does not pass through it is what is wanted, the model answers another question than the total effect, and the warning says so. If the graph is wrong, correct the graph.
