# Z0420 — a direction given to Fisher's exact test on a table larger than 2 × 2

## Meaning
`alternative: less` or `alternative: greater` was given to `fisher_test`, but the cross-table of the two variables has more than two rows or more than two columns. A direction names a side of a single odds ratio, which only a 2 × 2 table has; the exact test of a larger table has no direction (section 6 of the Language Reference; open decision #91). `two_sided`, the default, is accepted on any table.

## Common causes
- A variable with several categories, such as an industry code, crossed with a two-valued one.
- A direction copied from a call on a 2 × 2 table.

## Example
```
> wages.fisher_test(industry, female, alternative: greater)
error[Z0420]: `alternative:` of `fisher_test` applies to a 2 × 2 table, but `industry` × `female` is 12 × 2
  = help: the exact test of a larger table has no direction: drop `alternative:`, or keep two values of each variable with `where`
```

## Fix
Drop `alternative:`, or keep two values of each variable with `where` so that the table is 2 × 2.
