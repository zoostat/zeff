# Z0422 — `estimated: true` given to the Kolmogorov–Smirnov test with a distribution other than the normal

## Meaning
`ks_test` was called with `estimated: true` and a distribution that is not the normal family. `estimated: true` is Lilliefors' form of the test: the parameters are taken from the data and the p-value comes from Dallal and Wilkinson's approximation, which was fitted for the normal distribution alone (question 4 (c) of release 0.7.0). For any other distribution the test is defined only with the parameters given.

## Common causes
- `dist.exponential()` or `dist.uniform()` with `estimated: true`, hoping for a correction that does not exist for them.
- A truncated or mixed normal, which is not the normal family.

## Example
```
> wages.ks_test(lwage, dist.lognormal(), estimated: true)
error[Z0422]: `estimated: true` of `ks_test` is defined for the normal distribution alone, but the distribution is the lognormal distribution · meanlog: 0, sdlog: 1
  = help: give the distribution with its parameters and drop `estimated:`; a test of normality with the parameters estimated is also `shapiro_test`
```

## Fix
Give the distribution with its parameters and drop `estimated:`, or test normality with `estimated: true` on `dist.normal()`, or with `shapiro_test`.
