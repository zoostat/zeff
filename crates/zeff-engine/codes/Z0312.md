# Z0312 — a line ends with a dot

## Meaning
A chain step continues on the next line only when the next line begins with `.`; a line may not end with the dot.

## Common causes
- A chain broken after the dot rather than before it.

## Example
```
> wages.
error[Z0312]: a line may not end with `.`
```

## Fix
Begin the next line with `.step(...)`.
