# Z0209 — the record has no such field

## Meaning
`r.name` asked a record for a field it does not have. A record's fields are those of its literal, known before the script runs.

## Common causes
- A misspelt field.
- A field present in one record and not in another of the same list.

## Example
```
> {a: 1, bb: 2}.b
error[Z0209]: the record has no field `b`
  = help: did you mean `a`?
```

## Fix
Use one of the record's fields, listed in the message.
