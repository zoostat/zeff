# Z0414 — a term that the result does not have

## Meaning
A result method was asked about a term by name, but the fit has no column of its model matrix with that name. Term names follow section 7.2 of the Language Reference: a variable keeps its name, a category level is `industry=Retail`, an interaction joins its parts with `:`, a transformation is written as in the formula (`exper^2`, `log(wage)`), and the constant is `(intercept)`. A test's result has no terms at all.

## Common causes
- A misspelt term: `educ2` for `exper^2`.
- A level written without its variable: `Retail` for `industry=Retail`.
- A variable that is not in the formula.
- A term asked of a test's result rather than of the fit.

## Example
```
> m.std_errors(educ2)
error[Z0414]: `educ2` is not a term of this fit
  = help: did you mean `educ`? the terms are `(intercept)`, `educ`, `exper`, `exper^2`, `female`
```

## Fix
Write the term as the fit's table prints it; the message lists every term of the fit.
