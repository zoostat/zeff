# Z0414 — a term that the result does not have

## Meaning
A result method was asked about a term by name, but the fit has no column of its model matrix with that name. Term names follow section 7.2 of the Language Reference: a variable keeps its name, a category level is `industry=Retail`, an interaction joins its parts with `:`, a transformation is written as in the formula (`exper^2`, `log(wage)`), and the constant is `(intercept)`. A test's result takes no term on its methods: most tests have no terms at all, and the factorial `anova`, whose table has a row for each term, gives those rows through `estimates()`. `margins` raises the same code for a variable, asked for or held by `at:`, that no term of the model is a function of: it takes variables, not terms, and lists the variables of the model.

## Common causes
- A misspelt term: `educ2` for `exper^2`.
- A level written without its variable: `Retail` for `industry=Retail`.
- A variable that is not in the formula.
- A term asked of a test's result rather than of the fit.
- A term of the factorial `anova` table named on `p_values`, where `estimates()` gives the rows.
- A variable asked of `margins` that is not in the formula, or a term such as `exper^2` in the place of its variable.

## Example
```
> m.std_errors(educ2)
error[Z0414]: `educ2` is not a term of this fit
  = help: did you mean `educ`? the terms are `(intercept)`, `educ`, `exper`, `exper^2`, `female`
```

## Fix
Write the term as the fit's table prints it; the message lists every term of the fit. Inside a function, a parameter that holds a variable or text is the term it names, so `coef(on)` reads the term the parameter `on` holds. For a test's result, leave the term out, and read the terms of a factorial `anova` from `estimates()`. For `margins`, name the variable: the slope through `exper^2` is part of `margins(exper)`.
