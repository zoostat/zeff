# Z0307 — nested too deeply or a chain too long

## Meaning
An expression is nested more than 256 levels deep, or a chain has more than 1,024 steps. The limits keep every machine, the browser included, within its stack (section 3.8 of the Language Reference).

## Common causes
- A generated script with thousands of nested parentheses or chained steps.

## Example
```
> let x = ((((((((((((((((( ... )))))))))))))))))
error[Z0307]: this is nested more than 256 levels deep
```

## Fix
Split the expression into steps with `let`.
