# W1006 — an observation drives a coefficient

## Meaning
Removing one observation would move some estimate by more than its standard error (a DFBETAS beyond 1), so that coefficient rests on that row rather than on the sample (section 11.1 of the Language Reference). The fit is reported; the rows are named.

## Common causes
- An outlier in the outcome or a regressor, often a data error.
- A rare combination of regressor values that is the only support for a term.

## Example
```
> wages.reg(log(wage) ~ educ + exper)
warning: 1 observation drives a coefficient: removing it moves an estimate by more than its standard error (row 4,021)
```

## Fix
Look at the row; `influence()` (release 0.4.0, step 17) lists leverage, Cook's distance and studentized residuals by row.
