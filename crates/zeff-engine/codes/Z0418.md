# Z0418 — a seq that cannot be built

## Meaning
`seq(from, to, step)` could not make its values. Either `step` is 0, so the sequence never leaves `from`; or `step` points away from `to` (a positive step from 5 to 1, a negative one from 1 to 5), so it never arrives; or an argument is `NA` or infinite; or the step is so small against the range that the sequence would hold more than ten million values.

## Common causes
- Counting down without a negative step: `seq(5, 1)` has the default step 1.
- A step computed from data that turned out to be 0 or `NA`.
- A step in the wrong unit, such as 1e-9 over a range of a million.

## Example
```
> seq(5, 1)
error[Z0418]: `seq` cannot reach `to` with this `step`
 --> scripts/main.zeff:1:8
  |
1 | seq(5, 1)
  |        ^ the step points away from `to`
  |
  = help: the step has the sign of `to - from`: `seq(5, 1, step: -1)` counts down, and `seq(1, 5)` counts up
```

## Fix
Give the step the sign of `to - from`, make it non-zero, test a computed step with `is_missing`, and use a step of a size the range can hold.
