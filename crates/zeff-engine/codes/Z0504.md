# Z0504 — an instrumented variable that is not a regressor

## Meaning
`instruments: endog ~ instr` says how regressors of the formula are instrumented: the variables on the left of its `~` are regressors, written in the formula like any other (section 7.1). One of them is not in the formula, so there is nothing for its instruments to stand in for. The checker says so before anything runs, and writes the formula with the variable added.

## Common causes
- The endogenous regressor left out of the formula, in the habit of software that writes it only inside brackets: Stata's `ivregress 2sls y x (educ = z)`.
- A name spelt one way in the formula and another in `instruments:`.
- The regressor taken out of the formula with `- educ` and still instrumented.

## Example
```
> card.ivreg(lwage ~ exper, instruments: educ ~ nearc4)
error[Z0504]: `educ` is instrumented but is not a regressor
  = help: add it to the regressors: `lwage ~ exper + educ`
```

## Fix
Write the instrumented regressor in the formula too: `card.ivreg(lwage ~ exper + educ, instruments: educ ~ nearc4)`. A formula held in a binding, or one written with a range of variables, is checked when the statement runs.
