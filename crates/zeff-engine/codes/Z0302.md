# Z0302 — a bracket or quote is never closed

## Meaning
An opening bracket, parenthesis or brace has no closing partner before the end of the file.

## Common causes
- A `(` or `[` left open.
- A `{` of a record left open.

## Example
```
> print(1
error[Z0302]: this `(` is never closed
```

## Fix
Close the bracket where the expression ends.
