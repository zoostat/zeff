# Z0212 — a whole-number division by zero

## Meaning
`div(a, 0)` or `mod(a, 0)` with two Ints has no value, so it is an error: a whole number cannot be infinite or `NA` by accident. With Floats the two functions follow `/`: `div(7.0, 0)` is `Inf` with a warning and `mod(7.0, 0)` is `NA` with a warning.

## Common causes
- A divisor computed from counts that can be zero.
- A column of Ints divided row by row where some rows hold zero.

## Example
```
> div(7, 0)
error[Z0212]: division by zero: `div(7, 0)` has no value
```

## Fix
Test the divisor first, as in `b != 0 && div(a, b) > 1`; on a frame, keep the rows with a divisor: `where(b != 0)`; or divide Floats, `div(1.0 * a, b)`, and accept `Inf` with its warning.
