# Z0402 — not one of the allowed words

## Meaning
An argument takes a fixed list of bare words, and the word written is not one of them.

## Common causes
- A misspelt word: `robus` for `robust`.
- A word from another program: `hc` for `robust`.

## Example
```
> wages.summary(stats: [mean, median])
error[Z0402]: `median` is not one of the allowed words
  = help: did you mean `mean`?
```

## Fix
Use one of the words listed in the message.
