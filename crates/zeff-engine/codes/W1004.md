# W1004 — little variation left after the fixed effects

## Meaning
A regressor keeps less than one per cent of its norm once the fixed effects of `fe:` are projected out: it barely varies within the effects' groups, so its estimate rests on the little that is left and is poorly determined. The fit is still reported; the warning names the regressor with the share of its variation that remains, prints beside the result and is listed in the footer of a `report` table (section 11.1 of the Language Reference). A regressor that keeps less than one part in a hundred million is absorbed outright and dropped with a note.

## Common causes
- A variable that changes little within a unit over time, such as years of schooling, under a unit effect.
- A variable that moves with the time variable, such as experience or age, under a time effect.
- A group-level variable under the effect of that group, which the effect all but absorbs.

## Example
```
> wages.reg(log(wage) ~ exper + exper^2, fe: worker + year, cluster: worker)
warning[W1004]: little variation left after fe: worker + year: `exper` keeps 0.89% of its variation
```

## Fix
Use a coarser effect that leaves the regressor room to vary, drop the regressor, or add its group mean as a control instead of absorbing the group.
