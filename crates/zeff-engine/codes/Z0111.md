# Z0111 — the seed comes too late, or twice

## Meaning
`seed(n)` is set once per script, before any statement draws random numbers. A `seed` after a draw would leave the draws before it unrepeatable by the seed printed, and a second `seed` would make the script's draws depend on where each statement stands relative to two seeds.

## Common causes
- `seed(42)` written after a `draw` or another statement that draws.
- Two `seed` calls in one script, or one in the console after draws have been made.

## Example
```
> dist.normal().draw(2)
> seed(42)
error[Z0111]: `seed` comes after a statement that has already drawn random numbers
  = help: put `seed(42)` at the top of the script, before the first draw; the draws so far used the seed 1703721024
```

## Fix
Move `seed(n)` to the top of the script and keep one; to give one call its own reproducible draws, use `draw(n, seed: 42)`.
