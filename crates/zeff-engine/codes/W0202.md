# W0202 — a number with a fraction became NA under to(Int)

## Meaning
`to(x, Int)` accepts only whole numbers: a value with a fraction, or an infinity, becomes `NA`, and this warning says how many did. Nothing is rounded or cut silently.

## Common causes
- `to(2.5, Int)`: a number that is not whole.
- A Float column of prices or means converted to Int.

## Example
```
> to(2.5, Int)
NA
warning: 2.5 has a fraction, so to(Int) gave NA
  = help: `round(x)`, `floor(x)` or `truncate(x)` chooses the whole number first
```

## Fix
Choose the whole number first with `round`, `floor` or `truncate`, then convert.
