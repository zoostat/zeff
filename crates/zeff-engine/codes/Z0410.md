# Z0410 — arguments that exclude each other

## Meaning
Two options of one call ask for different things and cannot both apply: `round(x, digits: 2, sig: 3)` cannot round to two decimal places and to three significant digits at once. Zeff never picks one silently.

## Common causes
- `digits:` and `sig:` both given to `round`.

## Example
```
> round(123.456, digits: 2, sig: 3)
error[Z0410]: `round` takes `digits:` or `sig:`, not both
```

## Fix
Keep the option you mean: `digits:` counts decimal places, `sig:` significant digits.
