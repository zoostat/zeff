# W1001 — near-singular regressors

## Meaning
The regressors of the model, each scaled to unit norm, have a condition number above 1e8: two of them, or a combination of several, are almost the same column, so the estimates of those terms are poorly determined and small changes in the data move them a lot. The fit is still reported; the warning is printed beside it and listed in the footer of a `report` table (section 11.1 of the Language Reference).

## Common causes
- Two measures of the same thing, `age` and `birth_year`, both in the model.
- A polynomial of high degree in a variable of large magnitude, `x^3` with `x` around 1e4.
- Dummy variables that together nearly reproduce the intercept.

## Example
```
> wages.reg(log(wage) ~ educ + exper + exper^2 + exper^3 + exper^4)
warning: near-singular regressors: the condition number of the scaled design is 2.1e+08
```

## Fix
Drop one of the near-duplicate regressors, or combine them; centre a variable before raising it to a power.
