# Z0213 — a field was read from something that is not a record

## Meaning
`.name` without parentheses reads a record's field. On any other value it is a mistake, usually a method call missing its parentheses. A result is not a record: a test's result is read by its methods, `statistic()`, `df()`, `p_values()`, `estimates()`, `ci()`, `effect_size()`, `n_obs()`, `h0()`, `h1()` and `alternative()`, and a fit by term, `coef(educ)`, `std_errors(educ)`, `p_values(educ)` and `ci(educ)`.

## Common causes
- `wages.describe` for `wages.describe()`.
- `t.statistic` or `t.df` on a test's result, for `t.statistic()` and `t.df()`.
- `x.a` where `x` is a number.

## Example
```
> let t = dataset(wages).t_test(wage, group: female)
> t.statistic
error[Z0213]: `.statistic` reads a record field, but this is a TestResult
  = help: a test's result is read by its methods: `.statistic()`, `.df()`, `.p_values()`, `.estimates()`, `.ci()`, `.effect_size()`, `.n_obs()`, `.h0()`, `.h1()` and `.alternative()`; a method call has parentheses: `.statistic()`
```

## Fix
Add the parentheses for a method, or read a field of a record.
