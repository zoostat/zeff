# W0213 — an item runs against the rest of its scale

## Meaning
`reliability` found an item whose correlation with the sum of the other items is negative: people who score high on the rest of the scale score low on this item. Most often the item is worded the other way round ("I often think of leaving" among statements of commitment) and has not been reversed. Alpha is then too low, and can be below 0. The scale is not changed: the warning names the item, and the figures printed are those of the items as they were given. The same code says that an item has one value in every row kept, so that it has no correlation with the rest.

## Common causes
- An item worded in reverse that is not named in `reverse:`.
- An item that measures something else than the rest of the scale.
- An item that everyone answered alike.

## Example
```
> dataset(engagement).reliability([eng1..eng6])
warning[W0213]: `eng4` runs against the rest of the scale: its correlation with the sum of the other items is negative
  = help: an item worded the other way round is named in `reverse:`, as `reverse: [eng4]`; the items are left as they are here
```

## Fix
Read the wording of the item. If it is worded the other way round, name it in `reverse:`. If it is not, it may not belong to the scale: look at its correlation with the rest and at the alpha of the scale without it, which the table gives.
