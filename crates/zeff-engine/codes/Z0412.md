# Z0412 — a two-group test given one group or more than two

## Meaning
`group:` of a two-group test (`t_test`, `wilcoxon_test`, `proportion_test`) names a variable whose rows take one value, or three or more (section 6 of the Language Reference). The test compares exactly two groups.

## Common causes
- A grouping variable with several categories, such as an industry code.
- A variable constant in the rows that remain after `where`.

## Example
```
> wages.t_test(lwage, group: industry)
error[Z0412]: `group:` of `t_test` has 5 levels, but a two-group test compares two
  = help: `anova` compares several groups and `kruskal_test` ranks them; `where` keeps two of them for this test
```

## Fix
Compare several groups with `anova` or `kruskal_test`, or keep two of them with `where` before the test.
