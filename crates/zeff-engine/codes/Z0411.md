# Z0411 — sampling weights in a test

## Meaning
A test (`t_test`, `anova`, `chi2_test`, `proportion_test` and their kind) was given `weights:` whose kind is sampling, the default of `weight_type:` (section 6 of the Language Reference). A test's p-value rests on a sampling distribution that a design weight does not give, so the test refuses rather than print a p-value that means something else.

## Common causes
- `wages.t_test(lwage, group: female, weights: w)`, the weights meant as survey weights.
- Frequency or analytic weights given without `weight_type:`, which then defaults to sampling.

## Example
```
> wages.t_test(lwage, group: female, weights: w)
error[Z0411]: `weights:` of `t_test` are sampling weights, which a test cannot use
  = help: a regression with robust standard errors gives design-based inference, `reg(y ~ 1, weights: w, se: robust)`; frequency or analytic weights are taken with `weight_type: frequency` or `weight_type: analytic`
```

## Fix
For survey weights, fit the comparison as a regression with robust standard errors; for counts of identical rows or precision weights, say so with `weight_type: frequency` or `weight_type: analytic`.
