# Z0321 — `.` alone outside the formulas of `learn` and `tune`

## Meaning
A `.` standing alone as a term means "every other column", and only the formulas of `learn` and `tune` read it (section 7.5 of the Language Reference, release 1.5). Anywhere else it names nothing.

## Common causes
- `y ~ .` written as R writes a regression on every column.
- A `.` left over from an edit.

## Example
```
> let f = lwage ~ .
error[Z0321]: `.` alone stands for every other column only in the formulas of `learn` and `tune`
```

## Fix
Name the terms, with a range where they are many: `y ~ x1..x9`.
