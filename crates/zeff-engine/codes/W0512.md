# W0512 — an instrument fails the exclusion restriction in the graph

## Meaning
`exclusion_check` held an instrument of a fit by instrumental variables against a causal graph and found that the instrument is not a valid one by that graph. An instrument may be related to the outcome through the exposure alone. Here either a path joins the instrument to the outcome without passing through the exposure, the regressors of the fit held fixed, and the path is named; or the instrument is a descendant of the exposure, so that it carries whatever confounds the exposure with the outcome; or a regressor of the fit is a descendant of the exposure, and to hold a consequence of the exposure fixed makes any instrument invalid. The estimate of the fit then mixes the effect of the exposure with that other relation. The fit is not changed and not refused.

## Common causes
- The instrument acts on the outcome directly, or through a variable other than the exposure.
- The instrument and the outcome share a cause that the model does not hold fixed.
- A regressor of the model is a common effect of the instrument and of a cause of the outcome, and holding it fixed opens a path.
- A variable that the exposure brings about was taken for an instrument.
- A control measured after the exposure and shaped by it, kept in the model beside the instrument.

## Example
```
> let g = dag {
>     offer -> training -> productivity
>     offer -> tenure -> productivity
>     ability -> [training, productivity]
>     latent ability
> }
> let m = dataset(staff).ivreg(productivity ~ training, instruments: training ~ offer)
> let c = m.exclusion_check(g, instrument: offer)
warning[W0512]: offer does not satisfy the exclusion restriction: the path offer → tenure → productivity joins it to productivity without passing through training
  = help: an instrument may be related to the outcome through the exposure alone: add a regressor that closes the path, or choose another instrument
```

## Fix
Add to the model a regressor that stands on the path named and is no common effect on it, so that the path is closed; drop a regressor the warning says opens the path, or one it says is a descendant of the exposure; or choose another instrument. If the graph is wrong, correct the graph: the check is of what the graph assumes, not of the data.
