# Z0505 — a variable on both sides of `instruments:`

## Meaning
In `instruments: endog ~ instr` the left of `~` holds the regressors that are instrumented and the right the variables that instrument them (section 7.1). The same variable is written on both sides, which would make it an instrument for itself: a variable correlated with the error cannot purge its own correlation.

## Common causes
- The instrumented regressor repeated on the right in the habit of listing every variable of the first stage.
- A copy of the formula's regressors pasted on the right, the endogenous one among them.

## Example
```
> card.ivreg(lwage ~ exper + educ, instruments: educ ~ nearc4 + educ)
error[Z0505]: `educ` is on both sides of `~` in `instruments:`
  = help: the left of `~` holds the regressors that are instrumented and the right the variables that instrument them; `educ` belongs on one side
```

## Fix
Leave the instrumented regressor on the left alone: `instruments: educ ~ nearc4`. The exogenous regressors of the formula need not be listed on the right; they are instruments for themselves already, and one written there is moved to the included instruments with a note.
